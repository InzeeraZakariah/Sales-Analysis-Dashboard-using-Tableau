# Sales Analysis Dashboard – Tableau

## Overview

The **Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze sales performance across **regions, stores, products, salespersons, discounts, quantity sold, payment methods, and profit**.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into interactive visualizations to identify sales trends, regional performance, product pricing, and salesperson performance.

## Objectives

- Analyze sales performance across different regions.
- Compare product prices across categories.
- Analyze discounts across regions.
- Identify regional quantity sold.
- Compare salesperson performance.
- Analyze store-level sales performance.
- Understand payment methods and profitability.

- **Tableau**

## Dashboard Features

### Product vs Unit Price

A treemap showing the relationship between products and their unit prices, allowing quick comparison of product pricing.

### Region vs Discount

A comparative visualization showing discount distribution across different regions.

### Region vs Quantity Sold

A pie chart representing the quantity of products sold across different regions.

### Region & Store Location vs Total Price

A bar chart comparing total sales prices across stores and regions.

### Salesperson vs Total Price

A comparison of total sales generated by individual salespersons.

### Payment Method & Profit

Interactive filters provide analysis based on different payment methods and profit status.

## Key Insights

The dashboard helps users:

- Identify high-performing regions.
- Compare store-level sales performance.
- Identify products with higher unit prices.
- Compare salesperson performance.
- Analyze regional discount patterns.
- Understand customer payment preferences.
- Filter sales data based on profitability.
